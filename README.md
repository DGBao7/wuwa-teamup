# WUWA-Teamups

## Link

**A simple team-building tool for Wuthering Waves.**

WUWA-Teamups helps players find suitable team compositions based on their owned Resonators. Search, filter and select Resonators to discover potential teams without having to browse through long lists manually.

## ✨ Features

### 🔎 Search Resonators

- Search Resonators by name.
- Quickly find a specific character from the available Resonator list.
- Clear the search query to restore the list of matching characters.

### 🎯 Filter by Rarity

- Filter Resonators by **4★** or **5★** rarity.
- Combine name search with rarity filters to narrow down the results.
- Enable both rarity filters to display both 4★ and 5★ Resonators.
- Toggle filters on or off to adjust the displayed results.

### ✅ Select Owned Resonators

- Select the Resonators you currently own.
- Selected characters are visually highlighted.
- Update your selection at any time to explore different team possibilities.

### ⚔️ Recommended Teams

- Discover teams that match your selected Resonators.
- View each team's composition at a glance.
- Identify the role of each Resonator, such as Main DPS, Sub-DPS and Support.
- Compare team tiers and power ratings to help evaluate potential compositions.

### 💡 Potential Teams

- Explore additional team compositions and possibilities.
- View team members, roles and rarity tiers in a structured layout.
- Easily compare multiple teams without opening separate pages.

### 🧩 Unused Resonators

- Identify Resonators that are not included in a recommended team.
- Review your available characters and consider other team combinations.

## 🎮 How to Use

1. **Search or filter** Resonators to find the characters you want.
2. **Select the Resonators** you own from the list.
3. **Explore recommended teams** based on your selection.
4. **Compare team compositions** by their Resonators, roles, tiers and power ratings.
5. **Adjust your selection** to explore alternative team combinations.

## 🖥️ Tech Stack

- **React** — Component-based user interface.
- **JavaScript** — Search, filtering and team-building logic.
- **CSS** — Responsive layouts and custom styling.
- **JSON** — Resonator data and team composition data.
- **Vite** — Development server and build tooling.

## 📁 Project Overview

The project separates its interface, data and reusable logic into dedicated modules to keep the code organized and easier to maintain.

- **Components** — Search interface, Resonator cards, filters and team displays.
- **Data** — Resonator information and team compositions stored in JSON files.
- **Hooks** — Reusable state and data-management logic.
- **Utils** — Search, filtering, selection and team-processing functions.
- **Assets** — Resonator icons and other visual resources.

## 🎯 Project Goal

The goal of WUWA-Teamups is to make team building more convenient and accessible by helping players explore team compositions based on the Resonators they own.

Instead of manually checking every character and team, players can search, filter and compare potential compositions through a clean and straightforward interface.
